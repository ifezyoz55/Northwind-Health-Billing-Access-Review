# Northwind Health — Billing Access Review

A practical Identity and Access Management (IAM) case study focused on reviewing employee permissions, designing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and identifying access risks in a fictional healthcare billing system.

# Project Overview
This project assesses access assigned to six employees at Northwind Health. The objective is to determine whether their permissions align with their job responsibilities and recommend improvements to access governance.

The assessment covers:

- Review of existing user permissions.
- RBAC role design and access gap analysis.
- Segregation of Duties (SoD) and risk assessment.
- Individual user access findings.
- Remediation planning and preventive controls.

# Assessment Summary
The review identified excessive permissions and access combinations that could expose billing operations to financial and operational risks.

A proposed RBAC model was developed for four roles: Billing Clerk, Billing Supervisor, Finance Manager, and IT Support. The recommendations apply least privilege and separate financial responsibilities from technical administration.
